\# 📊 AI-Powered Business Intelligence Dashboard



\## 🔍 Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end Business Intelligence and Data Analysis solution built with Python.

It analyzes sales data to uncover trends, customer behavior, and future sales forecasts, enhanced with AI-generated business insights.



The goal of this project is to demonstrate how data analysis can directly support business decision-making.



---



\## 🎯 Objectives

\- Analyze historical sales performance

\- Identify key business KPIs

\- Segment customers based on value and behavior

\- Forecast future sales

\- Generate AI-driven business insights and recommendations



---



\## 📊 Key Metrics

\- Total Revenue

\- Monthly Sales Growth

\- Average Order Value

\- Top Products \& Regions

\- Customer Segments (RFM Analysis)



---



\## 🧠 Techniques Used

\- Data Cleaning \& Preprocessing

\- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

\- Customer Segmentation (RFM \& KMeans)

\- Time Series Forecasting

\- AI-generated Business Insights



---



\## 🛠 Tech Stack

\- Python

\- pandas, numpy

\- matplotlib, plotly

\- scikit-learn

\- statsmodels / Prophet

\- Streamlit

\- OpenAI API



---



\## 📈 Dashboard Features

\- Interactive KPI dashboard

\- Sales trend visualization

\- Customer segmentation analysis

\- Sales forecasting

\- AI-generated insights \& recommendations



---



\## 💡 Business Insights Example

\- Identification of high-value customer segments

\- Seasonal sales patterns

\- Underperforming product categories

\- Regions with highest growth potential
